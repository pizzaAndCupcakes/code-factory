# Goals

What the work is for. An issue gets a priority only with one goal label
(`factory/RULES.md`, "The queue"), so this page decides what the factory builds. Keep
it to one top goal and at most four under it. Changing it is the person's decision.

Replace everything below with your own.

## The top goal

One sentence, and the one number that reads it. Say where the number is read and who
can read it. Example: "Ten teams use it every week. Read: weekly active teams, from
the usage dashboard, by the person."

## The goals under it

| Label | The goal | Read by |
|---|---|---|
| `goal-reach` | People who need it hear of it | Visits and sign-ups a week |
| `goal-works` | It does the job for those who try it | First-run success, from the customer pass |
| `goal-foundations` | It stays cheap and safe to change | Time from spec to merge; incidents |

Keep `goal-foundations`, or a goal like it, last and honest: work the factory files
for itself lands there, and the weekly review reports its share of the week.

## What cannot be read yet

List each goal with no reading, and the issue that would give it one. A goal nobody
can read is a wish.
